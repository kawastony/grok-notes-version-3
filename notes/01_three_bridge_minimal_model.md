# Three-bridge minimal model

## Bridges side by side

| b | Name | R band | Phase | Flow |
|------|------|------------|-------|------|
| A | Active span | deep (R ≤ R_A) | Active | Fast feed; membership locked |
| P_1 | Transitional pause | mid | Pause | Medium feed; membership soft |
| P_2 | Stalled / hollow pause | shallow | Pause | Near stall; exit needs high α |

**One active, two pause.** Same flow law; different relative-depth band and lock rules.

## Pause triggers
1. R enters a pause band  
2. Early slope ∂_t W < ε for duration τ_stall  
3. Loud vs soft channel conflict > δ

## Transfer
b⁺ = Φ(b⁻, R, α) ∈ B_adm(b⁻,R,α)

High α required to leave P_2. Deep R favors lock on A.

## Falsifiable prediction
**Hysteresis:** leave A at R_leave; return only at R_return < R_leave (or with sufficient α).  
Observable: loop in early-slope vs R on worsen vs improve sweeps in residual-clean Forman data.
