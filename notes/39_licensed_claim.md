## Licensed
On residual-gated L=10 tube data, \(F^{\rm rel}\) is the operative predictor of early feed, and that predictive power is **local in \(v_2\)**, not global.

Fair sentence:

> Relative Forman well depth predicts early feed substantially better than the raw control \(v_1\) or frozen B/T/P labels. The effect lives in a bounded, patchy \(v_2\) window (onset between 1.80 and 1.85, exit between 2.15 and 2.20, with a visible miss at 1.95), not a filled strip and not a landscape theorem.

Mediation (n=65): \(R^2(F^{\rm rel})=0.439\) vs \(v_1\) 0.013 vs labels 0.035. Adding \(v_1\) or labels to the well changes \(R^2\) by <0.01. Partial Spearman of \(v_1\) after \(F^{\rm rel}\) is 0.04 (p=0.76).

Do not say “continuous band,” “uniform regime,” or “stable strip.” Say **patchy local regime**. \(R^2=0.44\) is dominant, not complete.
