## Interpretation

1. **H0 is not a DESI-only observable.** BAO constrains D_M/r_d and D_H/r_d. Without a CMB or BBN anchor on r_d, H0 errors of ±9–10 km/s/Mpc are expected. Do not treat H0 ~ 83 as a Hubble-tension result.

2. **Ω_m is the real DESI-only contrast.** φ sits ~0.02 higher than ΛCDM. Overlap is still large (~1σ). Direction matches Stage 1 Planck (φ also higher Ω_m).

3. **σ_8 and Ω_c h² are loose.** logA and τ bandwidth warnings are expected: DESI BAO does not pin amplitude or reionization.
